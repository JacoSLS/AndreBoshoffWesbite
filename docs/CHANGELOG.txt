HOW TO DEPLOY A VERSION
-----------------------
A) UPDATING THE LIVE SITE  (the normal case)
   1. Unzip the version on your PC first.
   2. DELETE the "data" folder and the ".htaccess" file from what you unzipped.
        - data      holds Andre's wording, services, gallery and reviews as he
                    has edited them in the admin. Uploading it wipes his edits.
        - .htaccess the live copy has the https/www lines switched on and cPanel
                    may have added its own lines. Make the listed hand edits
                    instead (see HTACCESS-CHANGES.txt in the version folder).
   3. Zip the rest again, upload to public_html, Extract, overwrite, delete the zip.

B) FRESH INSTALL  (new host, or rebuilding from scratch)
   Upload the whole zip unchanged to public_html and follow DEPLOY-FIRST.txt
   inside it.

Never upload a zip that is not in this folder.
